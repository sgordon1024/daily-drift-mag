UFO / UAP · REPORTED FEATURE

The Nevada jet that reported a shadowing aircraft.

Official record

Archive case · May 19–20, 2025 · records reported March 20, 2026

At 02:10 UTC, an FAA controller entered a report from flight PWA192: the crew said another aircraft was flying alongside it over Nevada. The log recorded no nearby radar target. Twelve radar files were withheld from the later public-records release.

The encounter appears in an FAA operations log obtained by John Greenewald’s Black Vault through a public-records request. In the log dated May 19, 2025, the 02:10 UTC entry records Gulfstream flight PWA192 reporting an object roughly 20 miles away, flying a parallel course near the SWR navigation beacon. The entry describes its appearance as fighter-like and records the crew’s report of flight level 310 and climbing.

The Black Vault published the records on March 20, 2026, alongside air-traffic-control audio. The FAA’s response letter says it withheld 12 radar files as sensitive security information.

That leaves a concrete report and an incomplete public record. The log establishes what the crew told controllers; it does not establish the object’s identity, measured performance, or origin. A radar non-detection alone cannot turn an unidentified aircraft into evidence of extraordinary technology.

PARANORMAL · REPORTED FEATURE

The “talking mongoose” left a hair sample.

Research / analysis

From the archives · case began in 1931 · ZSL account June 1, 2026

A specimen slide in the Zoological Society of London’s collection holds hairs attributed to Gef, the animal an Isle of Man family said could talk. The slide gives the story something investigators can examine.

In 1931, the Irving family reported noises behind their farmhouse walls, then a voice. They said their unseen visitor spoke several languages and called itself an elderly mongoose from New Delhi. The claims drew journalists and paranormal investigators. By 1935, purported hairs and pawprints were being passed to zoologists for examination.

The Zoological Society of London traced the hairs through its former secretary Julian Huxley to naturalist F. Martin Duncan. Duncan donated boxes of specimen slides in 1957. During later work cataloguing the collections, staff and volunteers found a slide holding hairs attributed to Gef.

ZSL described that rediscovery on June 1, 2026. Its crucial qualification: the hairs have not been genetically tested, and their appearance suggests a domestic dog, possibly a border collie.

The specimen is real. The talking animal remains a claim. What survived is an unusually tangible piece of a paranormal investigation.

Strange nature / new research

An underwater speaker drew coral recruits.

Hawaii / Kāneʻohe Bay

Scientists in Hawaii played recordings of a bustling reef through an underwater speaker, then watched where baby corals settled. Artificial structures nearest the speaker collected the most coral recruits, especially when coated with a living bacterial material. The experiments ran in 2023 and 2024; the new coral study was published October 8. The results suggest a way to encourage settlement, rather than proof that a damaged reef has been rebuilt.

Strange science / astronomy

Webb traced a radio burst to a small galaxy.

Early universe / FRB 20240304B

A radio flash lasting milliseconds has led astronomers to an unexpectedly small galaxy in the early universe. Webb identified the home of the farthest fast radio burst yet reported, dating it to when the universe was about three billion years old. The galaxy was roughly a thousand times less massive than researchers expected and busy making stars. Its youth supports a young, highly magnetic neutron star as one explanation. The burst was detected in 2024; the new findings concern its host and distance. Its exact cause remains uncertain.

Strange technology / space

NASA tests the glass telescope built for LISA.

Goddard Space Flight Center / future LISA mission

NASA is advancing a telescope made from a glass-ceramic material for an observatory designed to measure distance changes smaller than a helium atom. LISA’s three future spacecraft will exchange laser beams across a triangle 1.6 million miles on each side, looking for passing gravitational waves. The newly announced engineering telescope is the last pre-flight test unit before flight hardware. Its amber-colored material resists changing shape with temperature. LISA remains a future mission, slated for the mid-2030s.
